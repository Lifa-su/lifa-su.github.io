📖 Our Story

It started with a frustration most job seekers know too well.

I was job hunting — rewriting resumes late at night, tailoring applications over and over, and wondering whether my LinkedIn profile was helping or quietly holding me back. The tools I found were either too generic, too expensive, or too scattered. As a developer, I thought: I can build a simpler path.

That path started with free tools for resume writing, cover letters, interview prep, and LinkedIn polish. But over time it became clear that people did not just need more widgets. They needed a better sequence: where to start, what to use first, and when human feedback is worth paying for.

So the current focus of Lifa AI Tools is much simpler than the old “more tools, more pages” story. Start with the Job Toolkit. Use the free tools to move faster. If you want a sharper final pass, add Resume Review Fast Track or LinkedIn Audit Fast Track.

I'm still building this mostly solo — writing the code, shaping the pages, improving the workflow, and keeping the offer stack practical. The goal is not to build a bloated funnel. It is to help people create clearer applications, stronger profiles, and more confident next steps.

🎯 Our Mission

To give job seekers a clear path from rough draft to stronger applications: practical tools first, optional human review second, and no confusing maze of outdated products in between.
