About Lifa AI Tools

Free, open-source AI-powered tools built for developers, designers, and creators.

Our Mission

We believe powerful developer tools should be accessible to everyone. Lifa AI Tools provides 50+ free AI-powered utilities that run entirely in your browser — no signup, no data collection, no limits.

Every tool is designed to save you time and boost your productivity, whether you're generating QR codes, reviewing code, building resumes, or creating Docker configurations.

Who We Are

Lifa AI Tools is an independent project by a developer who's passionate about making AI accessible. We're part of the growing movement of indie developers building useful tools for the community.

How We Sustain

Keeping 50+ tools free requires resources. We sustain our project through:
