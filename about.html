📖 Our Story

It started with a frustration most of us know too well.

I was job hunting — rewriting resumes at 2 AM, tailoring cover letters for the 30th time, wondering if my LinkedIn profile was saying the right things. Every "AI-powered" tool I found either wanted $30/month or locked the good stuff behind a paywall. As a developer, I thought: I can build something better. And I can make it free.

So I did. I built a resume builder. Then a cover letter generator. Then a LinkedIn optimizer. One tool turned into seven. Seven turned into fifty. And somewhere along the way, Lifa AI Tools became something bigger than I expected.

Today, Lifa is a collection of 300+ free AI-powered tools — not just for job seekers, but for developers, designers, writers, and creators. Every tool runs in your browser. No signups. No data collection. No catch.

I'm still just one person building this. I write the code, design the interfaces, fix the bugs at midnight, and ship updates on weekends. It's not a startup. It's not backed by VCs. It's a labor of love from someone who believes the best tools shouldn't cost a fortune.

🎯 Our Mission

To make AI-powered tools accessible to everyone — regardless of budget, background, or experience. Whether you're a fresh grad writing your first resume or a senior dev optimizing your workflow, you deserve tools that just work.
