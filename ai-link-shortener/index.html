Sharing workflow

A simple sharing workflow that converts better

Shortening the link is only step one. The real lift comes from pairing the link with preview checks, better packaging, and offer clarity.

  1. Start with the destination that matters mostPick the page you actually want people to land on: product, portfolio piece, booking page, signup, case study, or campaign offer.
  2. Generate a cleaner short linkCreate a shorter URL that is easier to paste into captions, messages, decks, bios, and creator descriptions.
  3. Download the QR code if you share offlineUse QR for event slides, print material, postcards, leave-behinds, product packaging, or conference booths.
  4. Preview how the destination looks when sharedCheck the title, description, and image using AI Social Preview or AI Open Graph before publishing.
  5. Publish with the right CTAMatch the link to the audience: creators should move people toward content systems, while freelancers should move people toward service-ready assets and offers.
Best practices

Best practices for cleaner links and stronger click intent

Use one clear destination per share

Don’t bury people in too many options. Each post, email, or client update should drive to one obvious next step.

Preview before you publish

A short link can still underperform if the destination page has weak Open Graph tags or a bad preview image.

Keep recurring links accessible

Use history to keep your highest-value links close: toolkit pages, proposals, calendars, lead magnets, and offer pages.

Pair QR with context

A QR code works better when the slide, caption, flyer, or page tells people exactly why they should scan it now.

Shorten ugly URLs, not trust

Use shorter links to improve readability, but still give enough context around the destination so people feel safe clicking.

Build a repeatable publishing stack

Short link → social preview → CTA alignment → publish. That sequence is more reliable than improvising every time.
