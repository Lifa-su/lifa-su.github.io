Best practices

Small shooting improvements make the AI output noticeably better.

The cleaner the original input, the easier it is for background replacement and crop alignment to look natural.

💡

Before you shoot

  • Use soft front light instead of harsh top light or backlight.
  • Keep your face centered and your shoulders visible.
  • Avoid busy backgrounds, mirrors, and deep shadows behind your head.
  • Wear clothing that contrasts with the target background color.
✅

Before you submit

  • Double-check the exact size requirement from the receiving organization.
  • Zoom in to inspect hair edges, ears, and shoulder cut lines.
  • Retake the original photo if facial angle or expression looks off.
  • Save a second version in case one platform compresses the first upload.
