Sample Questions

A Taste of What's Inside

Behavioral

"Tell me about a time you had to deal with a difficult team member."

💡 STAR Tip: Focus on the specific action YOU took and the measurable result. Avoid blaming — show emotional intelligence.
Technical — Software

"How would you design a URL shortener that handles 10M requests per day?"

💡 Framework: Start with requirements, estimate scale, propose architecture, then discuss trade-offs and bottlenecks.
Leadership

"Describe a situation where you had to make a decision with incomplete information."

💡 STAR Tip: Emphasize your decision-making framework, risk assessment, and how you communicated uncertainty to stakeholders.
