📚 Negotiation Tips Library
🎯
Never Accept First Offer
Companies almost always have room to negotiate. The first offer is rarely the best they can do. Even a 10% bump compounds significantly over your career.
⏰
Take Your Time
Always ask for at least 3-5 business days to evaluate. Rushed decisions favor the employer. "I'm very excited about this opportunity and want to give it proper consideration."
📊
Use Data, Not Feelings
Back your ask with market data. "Based on my research, the market rate for this role in [city] is $X-$Y" is much stronger than "I feel I deserve more."
🤝
Negotiate Total Comp
If base salary is firm, negotiate signing bonus, equity, PTO, remote days, title, or review timeline. There are many levers beyond base pay.
💪
Know Your BATNA
Your Best Alternative To Negotiated Agreement is your power. Having another offer or being willing to walk away gives you real leverage.
📝
Get It In Writing
Verbal promises mean nothing. Always get the final offer in writing before accepting. "Could you send me an updated offer letter reflecting our discussion?"
