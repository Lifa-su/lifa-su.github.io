    FAQ

    Questions people ask before they use an SEO analyzer

    Short answers for creators, founders, and marketers who want to know when this tool helps and what to do next.

    What does AI SEO Pilot analyze?

    It checks titles, meta descriptions, headings, word count, image alt coverage, keyword patterns, links, canonical tags, Open Graph data, Twitter cards, and a few lightweight performance hints.

    Should I use this for blog posts or landing pages?

    Both. Blog posts benefit from stronger metadata, heading structure, and keyword coverage. Landing pages benefit from cleaner messaging, internal links, and social preview tags.

    Is this enough for a full SEO strategy?

    No. It is a fast first-pass audit. Use it to prioritize fixes, then pair it with keyword research, copy improvement, and content production tools if you want a fuller system.

    What pages usually improve first after using this tool?

    Thin landing pages, older blog posts, launch pages, portfolio pages, and creator resources often improve fastest because their basic on-page gaps are easy to spot and fix.

    What should I do right after I run an audit?

    Fix the title tag, meta description, H1, missing social tags, and internal links first. Then improve the copy and CTA so the page can rank and convert better.

    Where should I go next if I want a stronger content workflow?

    Start with Content Creator Toolkit, then use AI Copywriter, AI Social Post, and AI Keyword Research to turn insights into output.
