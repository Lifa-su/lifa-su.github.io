Step 2: Add your client's billing details.

Step 3: Add line items with descriptions, quantities, and prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this invoice generator completely free?
Yes, 100% free with no hidden costs, no signup, and no limits on the number of invoices you can create. All processing happens locally in your browser.
Can I export my invoice as a PDF?
Yes! Click the "Print / Export PDF" button. In the print dialog, select "Save as PDF" as the destination. This works in all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
Is my data safe and private?
Absolutely. All your invoice data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. No data is ever sent to our servers or any third party. Your financial information stays completely private.
What currencies are supported?
We support 15+ currencies including US Dollar (USD), Euro (EUR), British Pound (GBP), Chinese Yuan (CNY), Japanese Yen (JPY), Australian Dollar (AUD), Canadian Dollar (CAD), Swiss Franc (CHF), Indian Rupee (INR), Korean Won (KRW), Brazilian Real (BRL), Mexican Peso (MXN), Singapore Dollar (SGD), Hong Kong Dollar (HKD), and Swedish Krona (SEK).
Can I add my company logo?
Yes! Click the logo upload area in the "From" section to upload your company logo. It will appear on the invoice preview and in the exported PDF. The logo is stored locally and never uploaded to any server.
How do I save and load invoice drafts?
Invoices are automatically saved as drafts while you work. You can also click "Save Draft" manually. To load a previous draft, click "Load Draft" in the toolbar. For backup, use "Export JSON" to download your invoice data as a file.
Can I use this for my freelance business?
Absolutely! This tool is perfect for freelancers, small businesses, and contractors. Create professional invoices with your branding, payment terms, and multiple currency support. No accounting software subscription needed.
Does it calculate taxes automatically?
Yes. Enter your tax rate percentage and optionally customize the tax label (Tax, VAT, GST, etc.). The tool automatically calculates tax on the subtotal after any discounts are applied.
