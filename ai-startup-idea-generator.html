❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Startup Idea Generator work?
The generator uses a slot machine mechanic with three reels: Industry, Pain Point, and Business Model. Each spin randomly combines one item from each reel to create a unique startup concept, then generates a complete startup summary including elevator pitch, target users, competitive edge, market size estimate, difficulty rating, and a first-month action plan.
How many unique startup ideas can be generated?
With 60+ industries, 50+ pain points, and 30+ business models, the generator can produce over 100,000 unique combinations. Each combination also gets a unique elevator pitch, target audience, and action plan, making the actual variety even greater.
Can I save or share my favorite startup ideas?
Yes! You can favorite ideas by clicking the heart icon, and they'll be saved to your browser's local storage. You can also share ideas via Twitter, LinkedIn, or copy them to your clipboard. Your history of the last 20 generated ideas is also saved automatically.
Can I lock a reel to keep one category fixed?
Absolutely. Click the lock icon on any reel to keep that category fixed while spinning the others. This is great for exploring ideas within a specific industry or business model you're interested in.
Is this tool free to use?
100% free, no signup required. All data is stored locally in your browser. We don't collect any personal information or track your generated ideas.
Can I use these ideas for my actual startup?
Of course! These ideas are meant to spark creativity and help you brainstorm. Use them as starting points, combine multiple ideas, or let them inspire completely new directions. The best startups often come from unexpected combinations.
