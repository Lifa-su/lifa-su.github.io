Best practices

How to get stronger copy from AI instead of generic filler

Start with one audience

“Small business owners” is already better than “everyone.” Narrow further when possible: local gyms, Etsy sellers, indie founders, or real estate agents.

Write the desired outcome

Tell the tool whether the goal is clicks, replies, signups, calls, or purchases. Strong copy is goal-specific.

Add proof and specifics

Include features, pricing, turnaround time, key objections, or differentiators. Specific inputs create more believable outputs.

Generate multiple angles

Use 3 to 5 versions, then combine the strongest hook, clearest explanation, and best CTA into one final draft.

Edit for brand voice

AI gives you momentum, not the last 10%. Remove clichés, tighten claims, and make it sound like your business or creator brand.

Match channel to format

A landing page needs clarity and scannability. A social caption needs a hook. A client email needs relevance and a low-friction CTA.
