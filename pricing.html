Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before choosing your next step

What is the difference between Job Toolkit and LinkedIn Optimizer Pro? ▼

Job Toolkit is the broader paid bundle for applications and interview prep. LinkedIn Optimizer Pro is the smaller $7 product focused only on improving your LinkedIn profile.

Are these subscriptions or one-time purchases? ▼

They are all one-time offers. Job Toolkit is $29, LinkedIn Optimizer Pro is $7, Resume Review Fast Track is $49, and LinkedIn Audit Fast Track is $69 standalone or +$29 as an add-on with Resume Review.

Can I still use the free tools without buying anything? ▼

Yes. The free tools are still available with no signup required. The paid products and services are optional next steps when you want more structure, stronger LinkedIn support, or direct human feedback.

When should I choose Resume Review or LinkedIn Audit? ▼

Choose Resume Review Fast Track if your resume is the main blocker. Choose LinkedIn Audit Fast Track if your profile feels generic or misaligned. If both need help, you can start with Resume Review and add LinkedIn Audit for +$29.

How do the paid digital products work after purchase? ▼

Job Toolkit and LinkedIn Optimizer Pro are digital products. You review the product page first, then Ko-fi handles the checkout and delivery flow for the one-time purchase.

What is included in the human review services? ▼

Both services are focused written reviews delivered within 48 hours. They are not unlimited coaching, live calls, or done-for-you editing. Resume Review focuses on your resume and one target role. LinkedIn Audit focuses on your profile, headline, About section, and related positioning.
