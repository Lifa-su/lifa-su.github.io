Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before choosing your next step

What should I buy first? ▼

For most job seekers, start with the $29 Job Toolkit. It is the main paid starting point for resume work, cover letters, and interview prep. Add Resume Review Fast Track only if you want human resume feedback. For LinkedIn, start with the free AI LinkedIn Optimizer before moving to LinkedIn Audit Fast Track.

Are these subscriptions or one-time purchases? ▼

All paid offers on this page are one-time. Job Toolkit is $29, Resume Review Fast Track is $49, and LinkedIn Audit Fast Track is $69. The free tools remain free to use.

Can I still use the free tools without buying anything? ▼

Yes. You can still use AI Resume Builder, AI Cover Letter Builder, AI Interview Prep, AI Job Tracker, AI Skill Assessment, AI Social Bio, AI Salary Negotiator, AI Networking Email, and the free AI LinkedIn Optimizer with no signup required. If those free passes show you need a fuller reset, the next paid step is still Job Toolkit.

When should I choose Resume Review or LinkedIn Audit? ▼

Choose one of these only when a specific blocker remains after your own pass and the core toolkit work. Resume Review is for resume feedback on one target role. LinkedIn Audit is for a profile that still feels generic or misaligned after you have already optimized it.

How do the paid digital products work after purchase? ▼

The main digital purchase path here is Job Toolkit. You review the product page first, then Ko-fi handles privacy-conscious checkout with minimal data and instant digital access for the one-time purchase.

What is included in the human review services? ▼

Both services are focused written reviews delivered within 48 hours. Resume Review needs your current resume and one target job description. LinkedIn Audit needs your LinkedIn URL and one target job description. They are not unlimited coaching, live calls, or done-for-you editing.
