## 详细描述

### English

**Stop wasting time reading long articles. Get the key points in seconds.**

AI Summary uses advanced AI (powered by DeepSeek) to instantly summarize any web page with one click. Whether it's a news article, blog post, research paper, or documentation — get the essence in seconds, not minutes.

**🚀 Key Features:**

• **One-Click Summary** — Click the icon or press Ctrl+Shift+S to instantly summarize any page
• **Smart Language Detection** — Automatically detects Chinese or English content and summarizes in the same language
• **3 Summary Lengths** — Choose Brief (~100 words), Standard (~300 words), or Detailed (~600 words)
• **Summary History** — Automatically saves your last 50 summaries for easy reference
• **Export to Markdown** — One-click export for your notes, blog, or documentation
• **Keyboard Shortcut** — Ctrl+Shift+S (Mac: Cmd+Shift+S) for power users
• **Beautiful Dark UI** — Clean, minimal popup that doesn't get in your way

**💡 Perfect For:**

• Researchers scanning multiple papers
• Students studying online materials
• Professionals catching up on industry news
• Content creators gathering information
• Anyone who values their time

**🔒 Privacy First:**
• Your data stays on your device
• We only send page content to the AI API for summarization
• No tracking, no analytics, no data collection

**💰 Pricing:**
• Free: 5 summaries per day
• Pro: $3.99/month for unlimited summaries

**🛠 How It Works:**
1. Install the extension
2. Add your DeepSeek API key in settings (get one free at platform.deepseek.com)
3. Visit any web page
4. Click the icon or press Ctrl+Shift+S
5. Done! Your summary is ready
