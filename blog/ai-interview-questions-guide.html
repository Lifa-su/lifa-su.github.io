50 Common Interview Questions and How AI Can Help You Prepare

Interview preparation is the most underpracticed part of job searching. Most candidates review their resume and hope for the best. The candidates who consistently land offers are the ones who have practiced their answers out loud — multiple times — for the questions that actually come up.

This guide covers the 50 most common interview questions organized by category, with practical guidance on how AI tools can accelerate your preparation.

The STAR method: For behavioral questions (anything starting with "Tell me about a time..."), structure your answer: Situation → Task → Action → Result. AI tools can help you format your experiences into STAR stories.

Category 1: The Classics (Every Interview)

  1. Tell me about yourself.
  2. Why do you want to work here?
  3. What are your greatest strengths?
  4. What is your greatest weakness?
  5. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
  6. Why are you leaving your current job?
  7. What makes you the best candidate for this role?
  8. What do you know about our company?
  9. How did you hear about this position?
  10. Do you have any questions for us?

AI tip: Use the AI Interview Prep tool to generate tailored answers for these questions based on your specific background and the target role.

Category 2: Behavioral Questions

  1. Tell me about a time you handled a difficult coworker.
  2. Describe a situation where you had to meet a tight deadline.
  3. Tell me about a time you failed and what you learned.
  4. Describe a time you led a team through a challenge.
  5. Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager.
  6. Give an example of a time you had to prioritize multiple projects.
  7. Describe a time you went above and beyond for a customer or client.
  8. Tell me about a time you had to adapt quickly to change.
  9. Describe a situation where you had to give critical feedback.
  10. Tell me about your most significant professional achievement.

Category 3: Skills & Experience

  1. Walk me through your resume.
  2. What relevant experience do you have for this role?
  3. How do you stay current with developments in your field?
  4. What tools and technologies are you proficient in?
  5. Describe your process for [key skill for the role].
  6. What's the most complex project you've managed?
  7. How do you handle feedback and criticism?
  8. What's your approach to problem-solving?
  9. How do you measure the success of your work?
  10. What metrics have you been responsible for?

Category 4: Culture & Fit

  1. How would your coworkers describe you?
  2. What type of work environment do you thrive in?
  3. How do you prefer to receive feedback?
  4. Are you a team player or do you prefer working independently?
  5. What motivates you?
  6. What does work-life balance mean to you?
  7. How do you handle stress and pressure?
  8. What are your values in the workplace?
  9. Describe your management style.
  10. How do you build relationships with new colleagues?

Category 5: Situational Questions

  1. If you were given this project with no guidance, how would you start?
  2. What would you do in your first 30/60/90 days?
  3. How would you handle a situation where you didn't know the answer?
  4. If two priorities conflict, how do you decide what to do first?
  5. How would you approach convincing a skeptical stakeholder?

Category 6: Salary & Logistics

  1. What are your salary expectations?
  2. Are you interviewing with other companies?
  3. When can you start?
  4. Are you open to relocation / remote work?
  5. Do you have any concerns about this role?

How AI Transforms Interview Prep

Traditional interview prep: read a list of questions, mentally rehearse answers, show up and wing it. AI-powered prep:

  1. Generate role-specific questions: Input the job description, get a customized list of likely questions based on the role, industry, and company type.
  2. Draft STAR stories: Describe your experience in plain language, let AI format it into a structured STAR answer.
  3. Practice objection handling: AI can play the skeptical interviewer and challenge your answers.
  4. Optimize your "Tell me about yourself": The most important answer in any interview. AI can help you craft a 90-second narrative that's compelling and relevant.
  5. Prepare smart questions to ask: AI generates thoughtful questions that show genuine interest and strategic thinking.
The practice rule: Reading answers doesn't work. You need to say them out loud, ideally recorded, to hear how you actually sound. Use AI to generate questions, then practice answering them verbally — not in your head.

The Night Before Checklist

  • Research the company (recent news, products, culture)
  • Review the job description one more time
  • Prepare 3-5 STAR stories covering different competencies
  • Prepare thoughtful questions to ask the interviewer
  • Know your salary range and be ready to discuss it
  • Confirm logistics (time, location/link, interviewer name)
