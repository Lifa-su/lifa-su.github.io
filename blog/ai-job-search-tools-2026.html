3. Interview Preparation

Interviews are won or lost in preparation. AI can simulate realistic interview scenarios, give you feedback on your answers, and help you prepare for the toughest questions.

AI interview prep strategies:

  • Role-specific questions: Ask AI to generate 20 likely interview questions for the specific role and company
  • STAR method practice: Give AI your stories, let it help you structure them in Situation-Task-Action-Result format
  • Difficult question prep: "Why did you leave your last job?" / "Tell me about a failure" — practice with AI until the answer is natural
  • Questions to ask: AI generates smart, role-specific questions that show you've done your homework

4. Salary Research and Negotiation

Most people leave $5,000–$20,000 on the table by not negotiating. AI helps you research market rates and practice the actual conversation.

How to use AI for salary negotiation:

  • Ask AI to summarize salary data for your role, industry, and location
  • Generate negotiation scripts for different scenarios (initial offer, counter, benefits)
  • Role-play the negotiation conversation with AI as the hiring manager
  • Prepare responses to common pushbacks ("That's the best we can do")
