Understanding ChatGPT's Role in Email Marketing

Before diving into prompts, it's important to set the right expectations. ChatGPT is a powerful drafting and ideation tool — not a set-and-forget copywriter. The best email marketers use it as a collaborator: generating first drafts, brainstorming subject line variations, and overcoming writer's block, then refining the output with their own brand voice and audience knowledge.

The marketers who get the best results from ChatGPT are those who give it rich context upfront. The more you tell it about your brand, audience, and goals, the better the output.

Setting Up Your ChatGPT "Marketing Brain"

Start every email marketing session with a context-setting prompt. This acts as a briefing document that shapes all subsequent outputs:

📋 Context Prompt Template You are an expert email marketing copywriter for [Brand Name]. Brand voice: [e.g., friendly, direct, slightly witty — like talking to a knowledgeable friend] Target audience: [e.g., freelance designers aged 25–40 who want to grow their client base] Core offer: [e.g., a $97 online course teaching freelancers to raise their rates] Key pain points: [e.g., undercharging, feast-or-famine income cycles, imposter syndrome] Brand differentiator: [e.g., practical, no-fluff advice from someone who scaled to $15k/month] Keep all emails under 250 words unless asked otherwise. Always include one clear CTA per email.

Paste this at the start of your session and ChatGPT will maintain this persona for all subsequent requests — saving you time and producing much more on-brand output.

1. Writing Welcome Email Sequences

Your welcome sequence is your highest-impact email series. It's sent when subscriber intent is at its peak and sets the tone for the entire relationship. Here's a prompt for generating a 5-email welcome sequence:

✉️ Welcome Sequence Prompt Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers who downloaded my free guide: "[Guide Name]." Email 1 (Day 0): Deliver the freebie, set expectations, introduce my story briefly Email 2 (Day 1): Share my most popular blog post or piece of content Email 3 (Day 3): Address the #1 objection or misconception in my niche Email 4 (Day 5): Share a client success story or case study Email 5 (Day 7): Soft pitch for [product/service name] Each email needs: subject line, preview text, body copy, and one CTA. Tone: warm but direct.

💡 Pro Tip: Subject Line Testing

After generating the sequence, run this follow-up prompt: "Give me 5 alternative subject line variations for each email — mix curiosity, benefit-led, and question formats." This instantly gives you A/B testing material without extra effort.

2. Writing Promotional Emails That Convert

Promotional emails are where AI assistance pays off most visibly. Use this framework prompt for any sale or launch:

🛍️ Promotional Email Prompt Write a promotional email for a [X% off / $X discount] sale on [product name]. Sale runs: [date range] Urgency trigger: [e.g., price goes back up Sunday at midnight] Main benefit: [e.g., customers save $50 and get instant access to 3 bonus modules] Audience temperature: [cold list / warm subscribers who've opened before / past customers] Use the PAS framework: Problem → Agitate → Solution. End with a clear, single CTA button.

The PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution) framework is particularly effective for promotional emails because it connects emotionally before presenting the offer. ChatGPT executes this framework reliably when explicitly instructed.

3. Writing Re-Engagement Campaigns

Inactive subscribers hurt your deliverability and skew your analytics. A well-crafted re-engagement sequence can recover 10–15% of lapsed subscribers. Here's the prompt:

🔄 Re-engagement Prompt Write a 3-email re-engagement sequence for subscribers who haven't opened my emails in 90+ days. Email 1: "We miss you" — light humor, remind them of the value they signed up for Email 2: "Here's what you've missed" — a curated list of 3–5 best pieces of content or offers Email 3: "This is goodbye (unless...)" — clear unsubscribe option + compelling reason to stay Each email should be short (under 150 words). Make Email 3 feel honest and human, not manipulative.

4. Transactional & Lifecycle Emails

Don't neglect transactional emails — receipts, shipping confirmations, and account updates. These have the highest open rates of any email type (avg. 45–65%) and are prime real estate for cross-selling or brand building.

📦 Post-Purchase Email Prompt Write a post-purchase email for a customer who just bought [product name]. Goals: (1) Confirm the purchase and reduce buyer's remorse, (2) set expectations for delivery/access, (3) offer one relevant upsell or complementary resource without being pushy. Tone: warm, reassuring, celebratory. Include a specific "What happens next" section.
