The Surprising History of Lorem Ipsum

Lorem Ipsum is not random gibberish. It comes from "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (On the Ends of Good and Evil), a treatise on ethics written by Cicero in 45 BC. The text was scrambled and adopted by typesetters in the 1500s as a standard placeholder. The passage beginning "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet" has been the printing industry's standard dummy text for nearly five centuries.

Richard McClintock, a Latin professor at Hampden-Sydney College, traced the origins in the 1980s by looking up the unusual word "consectetur" in a Latin dictionary. He found it in section 1.10.32 of Cicero's work. The fact that this text survived from Roman philosophy through medieval printing to modern web design is remarkable — and a testament to how fundamental the need for placeholder text is in visual design.

Why Designers Still Use Placeholder Text

Some designers argue that you should always use real content. In theory, that is ideal. In practice, it is rarely possible in the early stages of a project. Here is why placeholder text remains essential:

Content Is Not Ready

In most projects, design and content creation happen in parallel. Waiting for final copy before starting layout work would add weeks to every project timeline. Placeholder text lets designers move forward while writers do their work.

Focus on Visual Design

When stakeholders see real words, they start editing the copy instead of evaluating the layout. Lorem Ipsum's unreadable Latin keeps attention on typography, spacing, color, and hierarchy — which is exactly what a design review should focus on.

Testing Edge Cases

A lorem ipsum generator lets you create text of any length to test how your layout handles different content volumes. What happens when a blog title is 120 characters? What if a product description is only two words? Placeholder text makes these tests trivial.

Classic Lorem Ipsum vs. Modern Alternatives

The traditional Lorem Ipsum text has a specific character and word distribution that does not match English. English averages about 4.7 characters per word; Latin placeholder text averages closer to 6. This means layouts designed with Lorem Ipsum may break when real English content is inserted because the text takes up different amounts of space.

Modern lorem ipsum generators offer alternatives that address this:

  • English-like placeholder text that matches real word length distributions
  • Industry-specific dummy text (tech, medical, legal, marketing) for more realistic mockups
  • Hipster Ipsum, Bacon Ipsum, and other themed generators for lighter presentations
  • Structured output with headings, paragraphs, and lists that mirror real content patterns

The best approach depends on your audience. For internal design reviews, classic Lorem Ipsum works fine. For client presentations where you want the mockup to feel more realistic, English-like placeholder text is a better choice.

💡 Pro tip: When generating placeholder text for responsive designs, create three versions: a short one (50 words), a medium one (150 words), and a long one (300+ words). Test your layout with all three to ensure it handles variable content gracefully.

How to Use a Lorem Ipsum Generator Effectively

Match the Content Structure

Do not just dump a wall of text into your mockup. Real content has structure — headings, short paragraphs, bullet lists, pull quotes. A good lorem ipsum generator lets you specify the output format: generate 3 paragraphs with a heading, or a bulleted list with 5 items, or a mix of short and long paragraphs.

Use Realistic Lengths

Blog posts typically run 800–2,000 words. Product descriptions are 50–200 words. Navigation labels are 1–3 words. Generate placeholder text that matches the expected content length for each element. This prevents the common problem of designing for 500 words of placeholder text when the real content will be 50.

Include Numbers and Special Characters

Real content includes prices ($49.99), dates (February 23, 2026), percentages (15% off), and email addresses. If your layout will display these, make sure your placeholder text includes similar patterns so you can verify formatting and alignment.

Test Internationalization

If your product will be localized, generate placeholder text in different scripts. German words are longer than English (Geschwindigkeitsbegrenzung, anyone?). Chinese characters take different vertical space. Arabic reads right-to-left. Testing with varied placeholder text catches layout issues before localization begins.

Common Mistakes with Placeholder Text

Shipping Lorem Ipsum to Production

It happens more often than anyone admits. A developer copies the mockup HTML, forgets to replace the placeholder text, and suddenly your pricing page says "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet." Always search your codebase for "lorem" before deploying. Better yet, add a CI check that flags Latin placeholder text in production builds.

Using Too Little Text

Designers often use just enough placeholder text to make the layout look good. Then real content arrives and it is three times longer, breaking the carefully crafted design. Always test with more text than you expect.

Ignoring Text Wrapping

Lorem Ipsum does not contain very long words (the longest is about 12 characters). But real content might include URLs, technical terms, or German compound words that do not wrap naturally. Test with overflow-wrap: break-word and verify your layout handles long strings.
